# Security policy

Report suspected vulnerabilities in Annabel, its packages, the starter, or the
CMS privately to the CodeMonster organization contact
[admin@codemonster.net](mailto:admin@codemonster.net). Keep vulnerability details,
credentials, and customer data out of public issues and pull requests.

Include the affected Composer package or application, its version or commit,
your PHP version, the relevant configuration, the observed impact, and the
steps needed to reproduce the issue. Remove secrets and personal data from
configuration samples, logs, and attachments.

For ordinary bugs and usage questions, use
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/codemonster-ru/annabel/issues).
